I am Junteng Liu, a first-year Ph.D. candidate at the **HKUST NLP Group**, Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), working under the supervision of Professor Junxian He. I graduated from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) in June 2024, where I completed my B.Eng. (2020 - 2024). My research focuses on natural language processing (NLP) and machine learning (ML).
